01 / ABOUT THE LAB

Building a future where technology includes everyone.

The Inclusive Futures Lab researches how technology can make everyday life more accessible, safe, and empowering for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD).

We connect disability services research with assistive and emerging technologies, including artificial intelligence. Our work focuses on developing practical technology training, cybersecurity awareness, and accessible technology practices.

02 / WHAT WE EXPLORE

Research at the intersection of people and technology.

Our work explores four connected areas that can help make technology more accessible, secure, and responsible.

01 ◈

Assistive & Accessible Technology

Exploring tools that support individual needs and abilities, including communication applications and augmentative and alternative communication (AAC).

ACCESSIBILITY
02 ⌑

Cybersecurity & Digital Literacy

Helping people with IDD and their supporters protect personal information, develop safe digital habits, and recognize potential scams.

DIGITAL SAFETY
03 ✳

Ethical Artificial Intelligence

Exploring responsible, transparent, and person-centered AI in education, support, and care, with attention to privacy, consent, and bias.

03 / OUR RESEARCH PRIORITIES

Turning research into meaningful support.

We focus on practical research that helps people use technology safely and confidently.

01

Technology Training for Supporters

Developing practical training for direct support professionals, teachers, families, and other staff—and studying how they transfer what they learn to the people they support.

↗
02

Safe Technology Use Across the Lifespan

Designing digital literacy and cybersecurity guidance suited to different life stages, from children to older adults with IDD.

↗
03

Ethical AI in Disability Services

Identifying guidelines for privacy, consent, and bias when artificial intelligence is used with people with IDD.

↗

04 / WHY IT MATTERS

Innovation should improve quality of life.

People with IDD and the families and staff who support them often have limited access to technology training, while facing risks such as scams, loss of privacy, and misuse of AI.

We work to build practical, accessible training so technology can strengthen independence, communication, and quality of life.

✓

Safer Digital Experiences

Promoting password safety, personal information protection, and scam awareness.

↗

Greater Accessibility

Increasing awareness of assistive technologies and communication tools.

◎

Responsible Innovation

Preparing support professionals and educators to use technology and AI responsibly.

∞

Stronger Communities

Building partnerships between higher education and organizations that serve people with IDD.
